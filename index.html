About IATA

A Multi-Department Ecosystem Designed for Sustainable Impact

IATA was co-founded by Edmore Taroiwa and Benjamin Nyamukusa to solve critical infrastructural, technological, educational, and ecological challenges facing modern Africa.

By integrating specialized leadership across Tech, Health, Education, Energy, and Food, IATA builds modular, high-impact innovations. From proprietary e-learning technology platforms to community health frameworks and green energy initiatives, we translate vision into functional systems.

"In all things acknowledge Him" — the guiding principle behind IATA's mission.

Our Mission Statement

"To engineer accessible, scalable, and cross-sector technology platforms that drive human development, health equity, knowledge sharing, clean energy adoption, and food security throughout Africa."
